About AI‑Analyst

A user‑friendly evolution of a complex spreadsheet—built to make company analysis fast, clear, and consistent.

From Spreadsheet to Web App

AI‑Analyst started life as a detailed Google Sheet that pulled, calculated, and reasoned about key company metrics. As friends and family began using it, the questions and navigation challenges grew. This site is the user‑friendly version of that work, designed to keep the core reasoning intact while improving clarity, consistency, and speed.

The Core: Score Trinity

  • Enterprise Quality (0–109): Operational strength and financial robustness.
  • Innovation & Disruption (−3 to 12): Technology, R&D, and category‑shaping potential.
  • Anti‑Fragile (−7 to 17): Resilience and ability to improve under stress.

The Overall assessment combines them (40% Quality, 35% IDQ, 25% Anti‑Fragile), normalized across ranges for consistency.

Company Page — A Guided Tour

  1. Compact Hero: When the hero scrolls out, a sticky summary appears (Logo • Ticker • Company • Price • Overall • Tier).
  2. Score Cards: The Score Trinity with details on click, helpful tooltips, and accessible explanations.
  3. Analysis: Succinct, structured insights that summarize strengths, risks, and outlook.
  4. Performance Trends: Theme‑aware chart for Revenue, Net Income, and EPS; responsive and legible.
  5. Key Financial Metrics: Curated metrics (P/E, EV/EBITDA, margins, growth). Aliases reduce “N/A”.
  6. Detailed Financials: Income, Balance, and Cash Flow tables for deeper verification.

Design Principles

  • Clarity: Consistent colors, spacing, and tooltips. Minimal surprises.
  • Performance: Lightweight, native web; responsive charts; accessible defaults.
  • Reusability: Universal header/footer, theme tokens, and services across pages.

What’s Next

  • Index Enhancements: Further polish for grid/list cards; performance for larger datasets.
  • New Pages: Comparison and portfolio views; richer analytics.
  • Better Onboarding: Explainers and optional tooltips for first‑time users.
